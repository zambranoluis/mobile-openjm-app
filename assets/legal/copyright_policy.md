# Copyright and Content Complaints

> Version 1.0 · Effective September 1st, 2026

Published by **Open Jamaica AI Limited**.

## 1. What this covers

OpenJM stores files that users upload and files that code execution generates. Those
files are private to the account that owns them and are deleted within 7 days. We do not
host a public library, feed or gallery.

This policy is how to tell us that content stored on the Service infringes your copyright
or is otherwise illegal, and what we do about it. It is our notice-and-action mechanism
for the purposes of Article 16 of the EU Digital Services Act.

We do not monitor stored content proactively, beyond the automated screening described in
Section 4 of the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable_use_policy.md).

## 2. Sending a notice

Email **copyright@openjm.ai**, or write to the address in Section 7. A notice must
include:

1. Enough detail to identify the content and where it is — an account email, file
   identifier, or the request that produced it. We cannot search accounts on a general
   description.
2. An explanation of why the content is infringing or unlawful, and of the right you hold.
3. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights
   holder, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and that you are the rights
   holder or authorised to act on their behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We will confirm receipt without undue delay and tell you our decision and the reasons for
it. A notice that meets points 1 to 6 gives us actual knowledge of the content for the
purposes of Article 6 of the Digital Services Act.

**Do not send a notice in bad faith.** A materially false notice may make you liable for
damages and costs, including ours.

## 3. What we do

If a notice is valid we will remove or disable access to the content and tell the account
holder, with a copy of the notice and the reason. Repeat infringement is a violation of
the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable_use_policy.md) and may result in termination under
Section 15 of the [Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md).

Because uploaded and generated files are deleted automatically after 7 days, content that
is the subject of a notice may already be gone by the time we act. We will say so if it
is.

## 4. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was wrong, email copyright@openjm.ai
with:

1. Identification of the content and where it was.
2. A statement that you believe in good faith it was removed as a result of mistake or
   misidentification.
3. Your name, address, telephone number and email address.
4. Your signature.

We will forward it to the complainant. If they do not tell us within 10 working days that
they have started proceedings, we may restore the content — subject to the 7-day deletion
schedule, which we cannot pause.

## 5. Appeals

Any decision we take under this policy can be appealed under Section 6 of the
[Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable_use_policy.md) — within 14 days, reviewed by a person
who was not involved in the original decision.

## 6. Model output and copyright

Output is generated by a model and we claim no ownership of it. We do not warrant that
output is free of third-party rights, and you are responsible for checking before you use
it. See Section 9 of the [Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md).
