# Data Processing Addendum

> Version 1.0 · Effective September 1st, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the
[Terms of Service](terms_of_service.md) between you ("Customer") and **Open Jamaica AI
Limited** ("OpenJM", "we", "us"), company number 1377772, registered
office 53 Lady Musgrave Road, Kingston 8, Jamaica.

## 1. When this DPA applies

This DPA applies where you use the Service as an organisation or in the course of a
business and, in doing so, submit personal data about other people — your end users,
employees or customers ("Customer Personal Data").

For that data **you are the controller and we are your processor**. You decide why and
how it is processed; we process it to give you the Service.

This DPA does **not** apply to your own account, billing, technical and abuse-prevention
data, or to content you submit as an individual for your own purposes. For those we are
the controller, and the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md) governs.

You do not need to sign anything. This DPA takes effect automatically when Section 1
applies. If your organisation requires a countersigned copy, email legal@openjm.ai.

## 3. Your responsibilities

You warrant that:

- you have a lawful basis for the processing you instruct, and an Article 9 condition
  where special-category data is involved;
- you have given your own data subjects the information they are owed, including that
  their data is processed by an AI inference provider outside the EEA;
- your instructions do not require us to breach data protection law; and
- you have the right to submit the content you submit.

You are responsible for what you send. We do not filter Customer Personal Data out of
your requests, and we cannot tell whose data it is.

## 4. Our obligations

**Documented instructions.** We process Customer Personal Data only on your documented
instructions, which consist of this DPA, the Terms and your use of the Service's
documented features. We will tell you if we believe an instruction breaches applicable
data protection law. Where a law requires us to process beyond your instructions, we will
tell you first unless that law prohibits it.

**No training, no other use.** We do not use Customer Personal Data to train or improve
models, and we do not use it for any purpose of our own. We do not sell it.

**Confidentiality.** Personnel authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by
confidentiality obligations and are granted access only where necessary. Access to
production content is restricted and logged in our audit chain.

**Security.** We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk
under Article 32. Those we describe publicly are in Section 9 of the
[Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md); the rest are available to you under Section 9 of
this DPA.

**Assistance with data subject rights.** The Service gives you the endpoints to serve
access, export and deletion yourself. Where a request reaches us directly, we will not
respond to it on your behalf — we will pass it to you without undue delay and tell the
individual we have done so. Where you cannot fulfil a request through the Service, we
will assist you, taking into account the nature of the processing.

**Assistance with Articles 32–36.** We will provide reasonable assistance with your data
protection impact assessments and prior consultations, and with your breach obligations
under Section 7.

## 5. Sub-processors

You give us **general authorisation** to engage sub-processors. The current list is
[Sub-processors](subprocessors.md), which is the authoritative version.

We impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective
than those in this DPA, and we remain fully liable to you for their performance.

**Changes.** We will give registered users at least **30 days' notice** by email before
adding or replacing a sub-processor that processes Customer Personal Data. You may object
on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period. If we cannot accommodate your
objection, you may terminate the affected part of the Service and reclaim your unused
credit balance under Section 6 of the Terms, as your sole remedy.

## 6. International transfers

We are established in Jamaica, which is not the subject of an EU adequacy decision. Two
separate transfers are involved, and they are covered differently.

are incorporated into this DPA by reference and form part of it. You are the data
exporter and we are the data importer.

They take effect when this DPA does, without further signature. If your organisation
requires a signed counterpart, email legal@openjm.ai.

### 6.3 Transfer impact assessment

We maintain an assessment of the laws of each destination country and of the measures
protecting data in transit and at rest there, covering in particular the United States
authorities' powers to compel disclosure. It records that Cloudflare receives content in
the clear and carries the highest residual risk, and that Backblaze receives ciphertext
only and carries the lowest. It is available on request at dpo@openjm.ai, and we will
tell you if a change in law or practice means we can no longer meet our obligations.

## 7. Personal data breaches

We will notify you **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a personal data breach
affecting Customer Personal Data, and in any event within **48 hours**. The notice will
describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects
and records affected so far as known, the likely consequences, the measures taken, and our
Data Protection Officer's contact details.

You are responsible for notifying your supervisory authority and your data subjects. We
will give you the information you reasonably need to do so.

Where the Jamaican Data Protection Act 2020 imposes a direct reporting duty on us in
respect of the same incident, we will also report to the OIC within 72 hours and will
coordinate with you on the content of both notices.

## 8. Deletion and return

Customer Personal Data submitted through the Service is deleted on the schedule published
in Section 3 and Section 8 of the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md) — prompts and
responses within 1 to 24 hours, uploaded and generated files within 7 days.

On termination of your account, remaining Customer Personal Data is deleted. Export it
first with `GET /v1/users/me/export` and `GET /v1/memory/export`.

Two exceptions, both required by law:

- Residual copies persist in encrypted off-site backups and age out within 30 days
  (transaction logs, 3 days). They are not accessible for any other purpose, and our
  restore procedure re-applies deletions.
- Audit-log entries and reduced billing records are retained as described in Sections 8
  and 10 of the Privacy Policy.

## 9. Audits

We will make available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA,
including our sub-processor list, our security measures and any third-party reports we
hold.

Where that is not sufficient, you may audit us, or appoint an independent auditor who is
not our competitor and who is bound by confidentiality, once in any 12-month period, on
30 days' written notice, during business hours, without unreasonable disruption, and at
your cost. You may audit more often where a supervisory authority requires it or
following a breach affecting your data. Audits may not extend to any other customer's
data, or to information whose disclosure would compromise the security of the Service.

## 10. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in Section 12 of the
Terms, except where the GDPR does not permit that limitation, including liability to a
data subject under Article 82.

## 11. Order of precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on the handling of personal data, this DPA governs.
If it conflicts with a transfer mechanism executed under Section 6, that mechanism
governs.
